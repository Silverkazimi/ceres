What Ceres keeps about you

Ceres shows public Splinterlands data. Looking at any account needs no login and stores nothing about you. This page lists everything the server may keep, for how long, and how to delete it.

Nothing is required

Browsing, routing and staging changes work without signing in. Your routing setup and staged changes are then saved in this browser only. Ceres sets no cookies and runs no analytics or advertising.

Signing in

Signing in is optional. Your wallet signs one short text with your Posting key. It is not a transaction, it costs nothing and nothing is sent to the blockchain. Ceres checks the signature against your account's public Posting key. It never sees or stores a private key. The session lives in the page only; refreshing the page ends it.

Deleting

Open the account menu (the wallet button in the header). Delete saved data removes your saved routing setup, staged changes and all their older versions from the server. Delete cached data removes the cached game data of your account. Both ask your wallet to sign a text made for exactly that deletion. Copies in your own browsers and devices stay until you remove them there; Ceres cannot reach them.
